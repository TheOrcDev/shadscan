## Monorepos, finally

Pointing the web scanner at a monorepo used to mean acquiring everything and
auditing whatever the root looked like. Discovery is now bounded and explicit:
shadscan finds the package roots that look like real apps, asks you to choose
one, and audits exactly that project — nothing else is fetched or retained.

## Bounded acquisition

GitHub archives stream through bounded source profiles that classify every
entry as it arrives and keep only what the audit needs. Small selected
manifests skip the archive entirely and fetch just their files. Limits are
reported precisely: the failed counter, the configured threshold, the observed
value, and the affected path.

## Durable queued scans

Scans too large for the inline budget are dispatched to a durable queue with
token-protected polling, and immutable results can be cached so repeat scans
of the same commit answer instantly. Source never persists either way.

## Ruleset 2026.07.33

The bundled ruleset grows to 56 rules, adding a high-confidence check that
inline icons and spinners inside shadcn Buttons carry position-correct
`data-icon="inline-start"` or `data-icon="inline-end"` attributes, while
icon-only controls stay excluded.
