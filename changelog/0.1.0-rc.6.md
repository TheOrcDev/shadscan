## The handoff, one keypress away

The recommended shadscan workflow is to hand the audit to a coding agent —
but until now the CLI printed a report and quietly exited, leaving the
handoff behind a flag most first-time users never saw. Interactive scans now
end with a short menu whose first option copies the paste-ready remediation
plan to your clipboard and prints it, so the next step is always visible.
Printing without copying, launching an installed agent, and adding a
pre-commit score gate sit right below it, and Enter still means "just the
score, thanks."

## Clipboard that works everywhere, honestly

Copying uses the platform's own utilities — pbcopy on macOS, clip on
Windows, wl-copy, xclip, or xsel on Linux — and falls back to the OSC 52
terminal escape for SSH sessions and minimal environments. When no channel
is available, shadscan says so and prints the handoff instead of pretending.
No new dependencies were added.

## Automation is untouched

The menu exists only for interactive terminals. CI environments, piped
output, --json, --prompt, and --no-interactive behave byte-for-byte as they
did in rc.5.
