## A menu you can feel

Typing `1` and pressing Enter worked, but it read as a form. The menu now
renders a live cursor: arrows or `j`/`k` move it (wrapping at the ends),
Enter confirms, and the row highlight follows you without the report above
ever scrolling. The old number keys still act as instant shortcuts, so
nothing you learned in rc.6 is wasted.

## One keypress to the handoff

The agent handoff is why the menu exists, so it starts highlighted — a bare
scan followed by a single Enter puts the paste-ready remediation plan on
your clipboard and prints it. Pressing `Esc` (or `q`) dismisses the menu and
keeps just the score, which the hint line below the menu spells out.

## Careful with your terminal

Raw-mode input is easy to get wrong, so the selector treats restoration as a
contract: raw mode, the cursor, and key listeners are restored on selection,
escape, interrupt, and error alike — Ctrl+C hands the terminal back before
forwarding the signal. Minimal terminals without raw-mode support fall back
to the rc.6 numbered prompt, `NO_COLOR` and non-unicode environments get a
plain ASCII pointer, and no new dependencies were added for any of it.
